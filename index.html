🚀 About SMCC TechQuest

SMCC TechQuest 2026 is a technology-focused campus event designed to encourage students to explore digital innovation, computer programming, and modern information technology.

This year's theme, "Empowering Students Through Digital Innovation," promotes creativity, collaboration, and technological learning among students.

The event features programming challenges, website development activities, networking demonstrations, digital presentations, and interactive technology workshops.

Students will have the opportunity to improve their knowledge, demonstrate their talents, and discover new possibilities in the world of computing.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. Who can join TechQuest?

Students interested in technology and computing can participate, subject to the event organizers' registration guidelines.

2. Is there a registration fee?

For this sample event page, registration is free.

3. Do I need programming experience?

No. Some activities are designed for beginners, while others provide challenges for experienced students.

4. Can I participate in multiple activities?

Yes. Participants may select multiple activities in the registration form.

5. Will participants receive certificates?

Certificate arrangements will depend on the official event organizers.
